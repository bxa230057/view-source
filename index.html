The web has become a place where cruelty is free.

I see it when people tear down someone's achievement post.

I see it when people argue repeatedly in comments.

This happens because people can hide, freeing them of consequence.

People put others down because they're unhappy and it's the easiest way to make themselves feel better for a second.

The people who pay for it are those who share with the world.

Negativity is free. Positivity takes kindness.
